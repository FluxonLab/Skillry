Since 3.0.0, third-party skills merged into a Skillry skill hub live inside that hub as `references/<former-name>.md` plus `references/<former-name>/` (and `scripts|templates|assets|examples/<former-name>/` when present), each with a provenance header naming the upstream, commit and license. Each project's content remains under its original license, reproduced in full here and kept alongside the content: as `community/<source>/LICENSE` for standalone content and as `references/<former-name>/LICENSE` beside merged content. Byte-identical files are pinned in `registry/community-source-lock.json`.

All redistributed sources use permissive licenses (MIT, and Apache-2.0 for one source). Sources whose terms do not permit redistribution, and sources whose license could not be verified from an upstream LICENSE file, were excluded (see end).
