# Code of Conduct

Skillry is a small, focused open-source project. We want it to be a welcoming, low-drama place to
contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask questions before assuming malice or incompetence.
- Keep discussion technical, specific, and on-topic.
- Respect the project's values: correctness, safety, and attribution over volume.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Submitting content you don't have the right to redistribute, or stripping attribution.
- Deliberately introducing insecure, malicious, or obfuscated skill/agent content.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other Skillry
channel — and to public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to FluxonLab via <https://fluxonlab.com>, or open a GitHub
Security Advisory if it concerns malicious content. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may
edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may ban repeat or
serious offenders from the project.
